# Analysis

## Overview

This folder contains the analytical work supporting the Victoria's Secret market research project.

The analysis uses Vividata cross-tabulations to compare Victoria's Secret lingerie purchasers with other relevant consumer groups.

## Analytical Framework

The project follows a comparative market research framework:

```text
Vividata Consumer Data
        ↓
Consumer Segmentation
        ↓
Cross-Tabulation
        ↓
Demographic Analysis
        ↓
Behavioural Analysis
        ↓
Attitude Analysis
        ↓
Business Insights
```

## Cross-Tabulation

The Vividata analysis was designed around multiple splits and nests to examine relationships between consumer characteristics and purchasing behaviour.

The analysis includes:

* Multiple consumer segments
* Multiple demographic splits
* Nested comparisons
* Purchasing behaviour
* Consumer attitudes
* #MeToo-related attitudes

## Research Questions

The analysis addresses questions such as:

1. What demographic characteristics are associated with Victoria's Secret purchasers?
2. How do Victoria's Secret purchasers differ from purchasers of other lingerie retailers?
3. Are there meaningful differences in consumer behaviour between the groups?
4. What consumer attitudes characterize the different groups?
5. Are attitudes related to the #MeToo movement different across the consumer segments?
6. What potential implications could these differences have for Victoria's Secret's marketing and brand strategy?

## Interpretation

The analysis focuses on identifying meaningful differences rather than simply reporting percentages.

A useful insight should answer:

> **What is different, why might it matter, and what could a business do with this information?**

Cross-tabulation results should therefore be interpreted in the context of:

* Consumer segment
* Variable being measured
* Size of the difference
* Sample size
* Business relevance
* Limitations of the research

## Statistical Considerations

The analysis is primarily descriptive and comparative.

Cross-tabulation results can identify associations and differences between groups, but they should not automatically be interpreted as evidence of causation.

## Business Insight Development

The final stage of the analysis translates research findings into potential business implications.

The process is:

**Finding → Interpretation → Consumer insight → Business implication**

For example, a demographic difference alone may not represent a useful business insight. A stronger insight connects the observed consumer characteristic to purchasing behaviour, attitudes, or brand perception.
