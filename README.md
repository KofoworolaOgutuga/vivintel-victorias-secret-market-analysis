# VivINTEL: Victoria's Secret Market Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes Victoria's Secret consumers using market research and cross-tabulation analysis from Vividata.

The analysis focuses on understanding how Victoria's Secret lingerie purchasers differ from purchasers of other lingerie retailers across demographics, consumer behaviour, and attitudes. The project also examines consumer attitudes related to the **#MeToo movement** and considers how these attitudes may influence perceptions of the Victoria's Secret brand.

The project was completed as part of a Business Analytics and market research course and demonstrates the use of consumer research data to develop business insights and support marketing decisions.

## Business Question

The primary research question was:

> **How do Victoria's Secret consumers differ from consumers who purchase lingerie from other retailers, and what consumer attitudes may help explain perceptions of the Victoria's Secret brand?**

The analysis considers:

* Consumer demographics
* Lingerie purchasing behaviour
* Brand and retailer differences
* Consumer attitudes
* Attitudes related to the #MeToo movement
* Potential implications for marketing and brand strategy

## Objectives

The project aimed to:

1. Identify characteristics of consumers who purchase from Victoria's Secret.
2. Compare Victoria's Secret purchasers with consumers who purchase from other lingerie retailers.
3. Analyze demographic and behavioural differences between consumer groups.
4. Examine relevant consumer attitudes using Vividata data.
5. Investigate whether attitudes related to the #MeToo movement may be associated with perceptions of Victoria's Secret.
6. Develop insights that could support business and marketing decisions.

## Data Source

The project uses **Vividata** consumer research data.

Victoria's Secret was identified through the Vividata category:

**Apparel → Shopping → Miscellaneous Products → Women's Clothing → Where Personally Bought/Past 12 Months (National)**

The analysis focuses on consumers who reported purchasing lingerie, with comparisons between Victoria's Secret purchasers and other relevant consumer groups where available.

## Methodology

The analysis uses **cross-tabulation and consumer segmentation** to compare groups of consumers.

The Vividata analysis was designed to include:

* At least four splits
* At least two nests
* At least eight questions
* Demographic variables
* Usage and purchasing variables
* Consumer attitude variables
* Questions related to the #MeToo movement

The analysis follows a comparative approach to identify patterns that may not be visible from demographic information alone.

### Analytical Approach

The general analytical process was:

1. Identify the relevant Vividata category.
2. Define the Victoria's Secret consumer segment.
3. Identify comparison groups.
4. Select relevant demographic and consumer behaviour variables.
5. Select attitude variables related to the research question.
6. Build cross-tabulations.
7. Compare results between consumer segments.
8. Identify meaningful differences and patterns.
9. Translate the findings into business and marketing insights.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Consumer Demographics

The project examines demographic characteristics of Victoria's Secret consumers and comparison groups.

Potential dimensions include:

* Age
* Gender
* Income
* Household characteristics
* Geographic characteristics
* Other available personal characteristics

### 2. Consumer Behaviour

The analysis considers purchasing and consumer behaviour associated with lingerie and apparel shopping.

This helps identify how Victoria's Secret purchasers may differ from consumers purchasing from other retailers.

### 3. Consumer Attitudes

The analysis also examines consumer attitudes that may provide additional context beyond demographic characteristics.

This is important because two consumer groups can have similar demographics while having different attitudes toward brands, social issues, or purchasing behaviour.

### 4. #MeToo-Related Attitudes

A specific focus of the project is the relationship between consumer attitudes related to the **#MeToo movement** and perceptions of Victoria's Secret.

The purpose is not to establish causation, but to identify patterns in the available consumer research data that may be relevant to brand perception and marketing strategy.

## Business Value

The project demonstrates how consumer research data can be used to move from raw survey information to business insights.

Rather than examining Victoria's Secret only through sales or demographic information, the analysis combines:

**Consumer characteristics → Purchasing behaviour → Attitudes → Brand perception → Business implications**

This type of analysis can support decisions involving:

* Target-market identification
* Consumer segmentation
* Brand positioning
* Marketing strategy
* Customer understanding
* Competitive research
* Communication strategy

## Limitations

Several limitations should be considered when interpreting the analysis:

* The analysis is based on available Vividata survey data.
* Cross-tabulation identifies relationships and differences but does not establish causation.
* Results depend on the variables and consumer segments selected for analysis.
* Some comparison groups may have different sample sizes.
* Findings should be interpreted within the context of the available survey data and research period.
