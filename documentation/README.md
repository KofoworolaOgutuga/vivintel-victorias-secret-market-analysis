# Documentation

## Overview

This folder contains written documentation associated with the VivINTEL Victoria's Secret market analysis.

The original academic assignment required an individual written analysis supported by Vividata cross-tabulation results.

## Research Context

The project examines Victoria's Secret within the lingerie and women's clothing market.

The research focuses on identifying differences between Victoria's Secret purchasers and consumers who purchase from other lingerie retailers.

## Research Objectives

The written analysis addresses:

* Who purchases from Victoria's Secret?
* How do these consumers differ from other lingerie purchasers?
* What demographic and behavioural characteristics are notable?
* What consumer attitudes are relevant?
* Are #MeToo-related attitudes associated with differences between consumer groups?
* What insights could be useful for business decision-making?

## Research Design

The assignment required:

* At least four splits
* At least two nests
* At least eight questions
* A focus on lingerie purchasers
* Demographic analysis
* Usage and behaviour analysis
* Consumer attitude analysis
* Analysis of relevant #MeToo-related attitudes

## Academic References

The original assignment used Vividata as the primary market research source.

Additional academic or industry sources should be listed in the project's final written report using the citation style required by the course.
