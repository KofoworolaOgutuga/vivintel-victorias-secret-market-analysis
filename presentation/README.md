# Presentation

## Overview

This folder contains the presentation materials developed for the VivINTEL Victoria's Secret case analysis.

The presentation communicates the major findings from the Vividata consumer research and connects the analysis to potential business and marketing implications.

## Presentation Focus

The presentation focuses on:

* Victoria's Secret consumers
* Comparison with other lingerie retailers
* Consumer demographics
* Consumer behaviour
* Consumer attitudes
* #MeToo-related attitudes
* Key market research insights
* Business implications

## Presentation Structure

The presentation follows a general structure of:

1. Business and research context
2. Research question
3. Data source and methodology
4. Consumer segmentation
5. Demographic findings
6. Behavioural findings
7. Attitude findings
8. #MeToo-related findings
9. Key insights
10. Business implications
